How often does it update?

Every 45 seconds, straight from the Karasu public API. Your numbers refresh at most once a minute, so a result shows up within a minute or so of the match ending.

How is “today” counted?

The widget keeps its own tally from your chosen hour, so matches, wins, losses and ELO today stay right even after more than five games. Leave the source in OBS and it carries on where it left off.

Does it cost anything?

No. Nothing to install and no account needed: it's a web page OBS shows over your stream.
